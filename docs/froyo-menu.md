# Froyo Foundry Menu

*Frozen yogurt for software developers. Fresh builds daily.*

## Flavors

Every flavor comes in every size. Prices are by size, not by flavor.

### Mint Condition

Cool mint froyo loaded with dark chocolate chips. Ships in mint condition, every time.

- **Dietary:** Made without gluten ingredients
- **Contains:** Milk, Soy

### Berry Blockchain Blast

Strawberry, blueberry, and blackberry sorbet, linked in an unbreakable chain of flavor. Fully decentralized sweetness.

- **Dietary:** Vegan, Dairy-free, Made without gluten ingredients
- **Contains:** None of the 9 major allergens

### Cookie Container

Cookies and cream froyo, packaged with everything it needs to run anywhere.

- **Dietary:** No special dietary tags
- **Contains:** Milk, Wheat, Soy

### Recursive Raspberry

Raspberry froyo with a raspberry swirl, with raspberry ripples inside the swirl. It calls itself.

- **Dietary:** Made without gluten ingredients
- **Contains:** Milk

### Vanilla Exception

Madagascar vanilla bean. The exceptional classic that never throws.

- **Dietary:** Made without gluten ingredients
- **Contains:** Milk

### Null Pointer Pistachio

Roasted pistachio froyo that points to nothing but pure flavor.

- **Dietary:** Made without gluten ingredients
- **Contains:** Milk, Tree nuts

### Java Jolt

Cold-brew coffee froyo with an espresso swirl. Compiles your morning in a single pass.

- **Dietary:** Made without gluten ingredients

### Peanut Butter Protocol

Creamy peanut butter froyo with a fudge ripple. The handshake every other flavor follows.

- **Dietary:** Made without gluten ingredients

### Cloud Caramel Cache

Salted caramel froyo, light as a cloud and served instantly from cache.

- **Dietary:** Made without gluten ingredients
- **Contains:** Milk

### Aicai Bowl

Açaí and banana sorbet, the only bowl trained entirely on fruit. Try it with Fresh Berries and Almond Granola.

- **Dietary:** Vegan, Dairy-free, Made without gluten ingredients
- **Contains:** None of the 9 major allergens

## Allergen notice

Our kitchens handle milk, eggs, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, and soy. All flavors and toppings are made and served on shared equipment, so we cannot guarantee that any item is free of any allergen. Fish, shellfish, and sesame are not used in any Froyo Foundry product. Ask a team member or see the Froyo Foundry Allergen Sheet before ordering if you have a food allergy.
